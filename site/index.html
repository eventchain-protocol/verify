What this checks, and what it does not

  • Each line is a well-formed entry, names the hash of the line before it, and carries a signature that verifies under the key on the line. A Mint entry's passkey assertion is checked too.
  • A valid signature shows that a key signed these bytes. Who holds the key, and whether the statement is true, are not in the file.
  • Payloads, key registries and time anchors are not checked here.
